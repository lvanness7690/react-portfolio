AI Prospecting & GTM Workflow

AI sales agent, outreach automation, and GTM systems

Overview

I built and operationalized a custom, end-to-end AI sales agent connecting ChatGPT, Instantly, MailerLite, APIs, multi-domain email infrastructure, and CRM workflows.

Problem

Prospecting and outbound preparation were manual, inconsistent, and time-consuming. The team needed a repeatable way to research and qualify leads, organize target lists, personalize outreach, manage sending operations, and preserve engagement data.

Workflow

  • Define campaign goals and target criteria
  • Use ChatGPT and connected APIs for structured company, account, and lead research
  • Build and qualify targeted prospect lists
  • Generate personalized sequence inputs and run outreach through Instantly and MailerLite
  • Manage multi-domain sending operations
  • Synchronize lead and engagement data with CRM workflows
  • Review output quality, sending health, and exceptions

Impact

The system reduced manual research and coordination while connecting list building, qualification, personalized outreach, sending operations, and CRM synchronization into one sales-enablement workflow.
